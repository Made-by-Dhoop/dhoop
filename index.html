// FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers on SEO, Google visibility, and how we manage your site.

What is the scope of website sizes you design and build?
We build websites of all sizes—from high-impact single-page landing pages and boutique portfolios to large, multi-page business platforms. Because every site is hand-coded from scratch, there are no rigid platform limits, plugin dependencies, or template restrictions.
Are your services exclusively limited to web design?
We started with high-performance web design and technical SEO, but our engineering capabilities extend beyond standard sites. We also build custom web applications, interactive digital tools, and bespoke software solutions tailored to your exact business workflow.
Will you get my new website listed and showing on Google?
Yes. Every website we build includes full Google Search Console verification, XML sitemap submission, clean URL architecture, and properly structured meta titles, descriptions, and heading tags.
How does hand-coded web design improve SEO?
Google uses Core Web Vitals and mobile page speed as direct ranking signals. Our hand-coded sites hit 99–100 Lighthouse performance scores, giving you a clean technical foundation.
Why hire Dhoop when anyone can make a site with AI?
Generating code is the easy 10%. Making a website commercially effective requires persuasive copywriting, technical SEO, Google indexing, DNS and SSL configuration, fast hosting, and ongoing maintenance. We handle every detail end-to-end.
Do you look after hosting, domains, security, and updates?
Everything is taken care of. We handle domain setup, fast hosting, SSL certificates, and uptime monitoring—and whenever you need text, photos, or new pages added, just message us directly and we update it for you.
